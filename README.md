# Freddy Voice Copilot

An AI-powered voice support copilot that connects customer calls with Freshdesk for intelligent resolution and seamless human-agent handoff.

## What it is

Freddy Voice Copilot is an AI voice support system that handles customer calls, understands their issues and sentiment, and connects with Freshdesk to provide relevant customer context to support agents.

## What it does

* Receives customer calls through Vobiz.
* Uses an ElevenLabs voice agent to communicate with customers.
* Understands the customer's issue and sentiment.
* Retrieves relevant customer and ticket information from Freshdesk.
* Attempts to resolve customer issues.
* Hands off unresolved issues to a human agent with conversation context.
* Assists the human agent with customer history, summaries, and next-best actions.

## What it doesn't do

* Completely replace human support agents.
* Automatically resolve issues that require human intervention.
* Make sensitive or irreversible support decisions without appropriate authorization.
* Act as a general-purpose voice assistant.

## Product Integrations

**Freshdesk** — Used for customer information, ticket management, customer history, and human-agent support workflows.

**Freshdesk MCP** — Used to connect the AI workflow with Freshdesk data and supported support actions.

**Vobiz** — Used for the customer-facing phone number and incoming voice calls.

**ElevenLabs** — Used to power Freddy's conversational voice agent.

**Anthropic** — Used for AI-assisted development and workflow implementation.
